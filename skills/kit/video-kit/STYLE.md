# Style guide — what makes a film feel directed

Read before writing the script. It is about craft, not about one look: the look comes from the user's creation.

## One visual language per film
- Pick ONE language (flat pages, particles and threads, real 3D scene, footage) and keep it from the first frame to the last.
- Languages may mix **inside** a scene when one serves the other (a real desk whose phone shows the app). Never alternate "old style page / new style page" from one page to the next.
- Different users must not get the same film: draw the palette, the typeface pairing, the camera family and the order of reveals from the user's creation and from a random seed, never from a default.

## Camera: a different move on every page, and it evolves
Vocabulary: push-in, pull-out, truck (sideways travel), orbit, crane up/down, drift (slow handheld-like float), whip, rack focus (blur moves from one plane to another), parallax (layers at different depths).
- Never the same move twice in a row; never the same move on more than a third of the pages.
- A repeated idea (several types of creation, several features) gets a **variation** of the move each time (same orbit, other axis and speed), not a copy.
- Ease in and out. Speed rises toward the reveal and calms afterwards.
- Every move has a reason: it leads the eye to the one thing the page says.

## Words
- Short sentences. One message per page. Verbs first. 2 to 6 words on screen when possible.
- No generic phrases ("simple, fast, modern"): say the precise thing the creation does.
- When the film lists kinds of things (types of creation, modes), say each name out loud and show it on screen at the same moment.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds; one call to action; nothing invented.

## Rhythm and sound
- Cut or change on the beat of the track; hold the key line 1.5 s longer than feels natural.
- Silence before the reveal, sound design under every transition (whoosh, pluck, ping), a low room tone so cuts never fall into dead air.

## Ending
- Finish on the brand or the creation's name, large, alone, held for about 2 seconds. Never end on a wide "overview" of the scene.

## Check before delivering
Contact sheet of 1 frame per second: do two neighbouring pages look alike? is any page empty for more than a beat? is the last frame the name? Fix once, then deliver.
